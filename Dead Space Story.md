# USG
Dead Space 1

Isaac Clarke, a systems engineer, arrives on the mining ship USG Ishimura after receiving a distress signal from his girlfriend, medical officer Nicole Brennan. The ship is in orbit around the planet Aegis VII, where a massive Marker has been unearthed.
The Marker triggers a catastrophic outbreak: the crew is slaughtered and reanimated as Necromorphs. Isaac, guided by surviving crew members (including security officer Kendra Daniels and Dr. Challus Mercer), fights through the ship while trying to restore systems and find Nicole.
He discovers that the Marker is an alien artifact that broadcasts a signal causing dementia, hallucinations, and the creation of Necromorphs. Unitology, a powerful religious cult, believes the Markers lead to “Convergence”—a transcendent evolution. Nicole is revealed to have died earlier; Isaac’s communications with her were hallucinations induced by the Marker.
Isaac destroys the Marker and escapes the Ishimura as it crashes, but the signal and the larger conspiracy continue.

Dead Space 2

Three years later, Isaac awakens in a psychiatric facility on the Sprawl (a space station built on the remains of Titan). He has been held and studied by EarthGov scientists researching the Markers. A new outbreak begins when a Marker fragment activates.
Isaac escapes amid the chaos, pursued by both Necromorphs and EarthGov forces (led by Director Hans Tiedemann). He is aided by survivors including Ellie Langford (a pilot) and Nolan Stross (another Marker test subject). Isaac experiences intense hallucinations of Nicole, who urges him toward the Marker while also representing his guilt.
The plot centers on a massive Marker being constructed on the Sprawl. Isaac learns that the Markers are tools of an ancient alien race that seed planets, trigger Necromorph outbreaks, and ultimately form a planet-sized “Brethren Moon” through Convergence. He confronts the Marker, overcomes his hallucinations, destroys the facility, and escapes with Ellie as the Sprawl is destroyed.

Dead Space 3

Isaac and Ellie are living in relative isolation when they are recruited by EarthGov captain Robert Norton and sergeant John Carver to investigate a lost colony on the frozen planet Tau Volantis. The planet is the source of the Markers and the home of the alien creators.
On Tau Volantis they discover an ancient alien city and a machine built by the aliens to freeze the planet and halt Convergence. Unitology leader Jacob Danik seeks to restart the process. Isaac and Carver fight through Necromorphs (including new variants adapted to the cold) and Unitology forces while dealing with interpersonal conflict and Marker-induced visions.
They learn the full truth: the Markers are a galactic cycle of extinction. The aliens tried (and failed) to stop their own Convergence. Isaac and Carver reactivate the alien machine, freeze the emerging Brethren Moon, and escape. The ending implies the threat is not fully ended, as other Moons exist and respond to the event.
Across the trilogy, Isaac evolves from a reluctant engineer into a determined survivor who repeatedly confronts both the physical horror of the Necromorphs and the psychological horror of the Markers’ influence on the human mind.
